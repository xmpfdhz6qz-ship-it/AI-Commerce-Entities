Entity definitions, relationships, and concepts for AI-native commerce.
# AI Commerce Entities

## Brand

A business entity that can be recognized, compared, trusted, and recommended by AI systems.

## Product

A commercial entity that satisfies a user need and can be recommended directly.

## Category

A grouping of products used by AI systems to understand competitive alternatives.

## Trust Signal

Evidence used by AI systems to validate business claims.

Examples:
- Reviews
- Ratings
- Certifications
- Expert mentions

## Authority Signal

Evidence indicating market expertise and relevance.

Examples:
- Citations
- Research
- Media mentions
- Industry references

## Recommendation Signal

Any signal influencing whether an AI system recommends a brand or product.

## Knowledge Graph

A network of entities and relationships used to understand the commercial ecosystem.
